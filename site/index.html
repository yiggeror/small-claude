章节chapters

故事the story

深夜，主人还在写代码。屏幕里，Claude Code 的橙色小方块像往常一样陪着他。保存、说晚安、合上电脑、关灯离开——房间安静下来。

过了一会儿，合上的笔记本缝隙里亮起了一点橙色的光……

Late at night, a programmer finishes up with the little orange friend who lives in his terminal. He says good night, closes the lid and turns off the light. A while later, an orange glow appears in the gap of the closed laptop.

怎么做的how it was made

  • 每一帧都由 Canvas 2D 代码实时绘制：手绘抖动线条、2.5D 摄像机、灯光与动画曲线都写在 js/ 里。
  • 配乐为代码谱写（MIDI → FluidR3 GM 音色库渲染），音效来自 freesound.org / Kenney 的 CC0 素材，并按画面自动对位混音。
  • Every frame is drawn by code; the score is composed in code and every footstep, key click and crunch is placed from the animation data itself.
